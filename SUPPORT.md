## What we sell

Small local-first software products (see the [product list](README.md)):
Chrome extensions such as Table Exporter, the CSV Viewer app and the PDF batch-to-Excel tool.
Each product has a free version. Where a **Pro license** is offered, it unlocks extra features
and is sold as a digital license (one-time purchase or subscription, as shown at checkout).
No physical goods are shipped.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Your card statement shows **NISHIAI**.

## Refund policy

- **14-day money-back guarantee.** If a Pro license does not work for you, email us within
  14 days of purchase and we refund the full amount — no questions asked.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days
  (depending on your bank).
- After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion, for example if a product stops working
  because of a defect we cannot fix.

## Cancellation (subscriptions)

You can cancel a subscription at any time from the link in your purchase email, or by
emailing us. Cancellation stops future charges; you keep Pro access until the end of the
period already paid.

## Disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank — most issues are solved
within a day.

## Privacy

The products do not collect personal data; processing happens locally on your device.
See each product's privacy policy, e.g. [Table Exporter](store/table-exporter/PRIVACY.md).
