I will convert scanned PDFs and images into accurate Excel or Word files

**Category:** Data → Data Entry → Data Conversion

**Search tags:** pdf to excel, data entry, pdf to word, scanned pdf, data conversion

**Description:**

Need the tables, forms or text from scanned PDFs, photos or image-only files turned into clean, editable Excel or Word documents? I deliver files you can sort, filter and calculate with, not just copied text.

**What you get**
- Excel workbooks with real numbers (not text), proper columns and totals
- Word documents that keep headings, lists and paragraph order
- A built-in accuracy check: every row is recalculated (quantity × price, column sums) and compared with the totals printed on your document. Any mismatch is highlighted, never hidden
- Unreadable cells are marked [UNREADABLE] and listed. I never guess numbers

**Good fit**
- Scanned invoices, price lists, inventories, bank-style tables, survey forms
- Fillable PDF forms collected in bulk → one Excel sheet
- Handwritten or printed pages → editable Word / Google Docs

**How I work**
I use AI-assisted reading plus a second, independent verification pass and arithmetic checks. If your project must be done without any AI or OCR tools, please tell me before ordering and I will decline, so you can choose another seller.

**Before ordering**, send me a sample page so I can confirm readability and quote exactly.

**FAQ**
- *Do you use OCR or AI?* Yes, AI-assisted reading, always followed by a separate verification pass and arithmetic checks. If you require purely manual work, this gig is not for you.
- *What if a page is hard to read?* Unclear values are flagged [UNREADABLE] with the page and cell, and I ask you rather than guess.
- *What happens to my files?* They are used only for your order. The work is AI-assisted, so your files are processed by the AI provider (Anthropic's Claude). Your files are not used for AI training. After delivery I delete the work conversation, and the provider removes it from its systems within 30 days. Please don't send documents with sensitive personal data (ID, medical or personal financial records).
- *Which languages?* English and Romanian documents; other Latin-script languages on request after a sample.

**Buyer requirements (questions at order):**
1. Upload the files (PDF, JPG, PNG).
2. Excel, Word or both? Any template or column order to follow?
3. Number format (1,234.56 or 1.234,56)?
