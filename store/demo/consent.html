Three slow days in the Apuseni Mountains

Demo article · 6 min read

The road climbs out of the valley in long, patient bends. By the second morning the village bakery knew our order, and the fog sat in the beech woods until almost noon.

We walked to the karst springs, lost the trail twice, and found it again thanks to a shepherd who pointed with his whole arm, the way people do when the answer is obvious to them.
