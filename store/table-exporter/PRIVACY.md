# Privacy policy — Table Exporter

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Table Exporter is a browser extension published by NISHIAI (Alexandru Stanciu, Timișoara, Romania).

## What the extension accesses
When you click the extension icon on a page, it reads the text of the HTML tables on that page
in order to build a CSV file. It does not read pages you have not clicked it on.

## What the extension collects
Nothing. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data,
browsing history or page content. There are no analytics, no tracking and no account.

## Where processing happens
Entirely inside your browser. The CSV file is created locally and saved through your browser's
normal download, or copied to your clipboard when you choose "Copy to clipboard".

## Third parties
None. The extension makes no network requests.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/Ramanul/nishiai-apps/issues

## Politica de confidențialitate (RO)

Table Exporter citește textul tabelelor HTML doar de pe pagina pe care apeși iconița, ca să construiască
fișierul CSV. Nu colectează, nu stochează, nu transmite și nu vinde date personale, istoric de navigare
sau conținut. Nu face nicio cerere în rețea; totul se întâmplă local, în browserul tău.
