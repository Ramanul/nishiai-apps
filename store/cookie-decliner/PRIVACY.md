# Privacy policy — Consent Decliner

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Consent Decliner is a browser extension published by NISHIAI (Alexandru Stanciu, Timișoara, Romania).

## What the extension accesses
When enabled, it looks at the cookie consent banner on the pages you open, to find and click the
site's own "Reject all" (or equivalent) button. It does not read or keep any other page content.

## What the extension stores
Only two values, in your browser's local extension storage: whether you turned the extension on,
and how many banners it has handled. Nothing else.

## What the extension collects
Nothing. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data or browsing history.
There are no analytics, no tracking, no account and no network requests.
